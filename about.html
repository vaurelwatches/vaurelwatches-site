About Vaurel

Time, in a Higher Form

An independent watch brand built around one idea: a watch should let you see its heart.

Our Beginning

It started with a single line.

Vaurel was founded in 2026 out of a simple frustration. Most dress watches hide everything interesting about how they work, and most watches that show their mechanics are loud. We wanted both at once: a watch elegant enough to wear with a suit, with a quiet surprise for anyone who looks closer.

The answer was the V. Cut into every dial, it opens a window onto the automatic movement, so the heart of the watch is always in view. Around it we kept everything else restrained: a clean rectangular case, slim baton markers and a single V at twelve o’clock.

Our Craft

Designed with intention. Made to be worn.

Every Vaurel runs on an automatic movement, wound by the motion of your wrist. Turn the watch over and a clear case back shows the movement from the other side, while the V returns on the crown and on the clasp.

Each design comes in men’s and women’s collections, in polished stainless steel or a warm rose gold tone, on genuine leather straps chosen to match every dial: emerald, midnight blue, obsidian, espresso, blush and rose.
